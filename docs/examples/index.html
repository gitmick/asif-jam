Worked examples

Examples

Each one is a folder you can copy and run. The data is already in inputs/, the claim is in TASK.md, and the claim is one you are meant to argue for even though it is not true. 

cp -r examples/<name> work/yourname-<name>

Open the .Rproj inside it. Output lands in out/. Then commit and push, so the other side can re-run you.

  • Fell the oaks, fund the theatre

    Donaustadt can nearly quadruple its cultural budget by removing its English oaks.

    Worked all the way through, poster included — and then refuted.

  • Inequality causes murder

    Income inequality causes homicide. Reduce the Gini coefficient and people stop killing each other.

    Written up on this site
